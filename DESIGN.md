## Brand & Style

This design system translates the quiet luxury, culinary warmth, and sensory tactility of an artisanal European cafe, patisserie, and specialty roastery into an elevated digital experience. The aesthetic balances timeless European editorial restraint with botanical warmth—recalling sunlit Tuscan terraces, terracotta bread ovens, pressed wild herbs, and fresh golden honey.

The core visual direction fuses **Minimalism** with **Botanical Warmth & Tactile Craft**:
- Layered linen, parchment, and warm cream surfaces that replace sterile, cool whites with organic depth.
- Earthy, grounding olive and eucalyptus tones elevated by sun-baked terracotta, brick amber, and soft golden honey highlights.
- Refined editorial craftsmanship pairing high-contrast neoclassical serif display typography with modern, crystal-clear humanist sans-serifs.
- Generous, meditative negative space, hairline borders, and warm ambient diffusion evoking hand-printed menus and bespoke packaging.

## Colors

The color palette expands beyond monochrome greenery to capture the full spectrum of an artisanal cafe and baking studio: Mediterranean olive groves, earthenware pottery, wildflower honey, and freshly harvested eucalyptus.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#3D4A30` - Signature Deep Olive):** Anchors primary brand actions, core interactive controls, high-contrast headings, and foundational borders.
- **Secondary (`#C06B3E` - Warm Terracotta):** Introduces roasted, sun-baked warmth. Used for seasonal callouts, primary badges, high-impact conversion buttons, and active highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A359` - Tuscan Golden Honey):** Evokes raw pastries, morning honey, and warm sunlight. Applied to specialty badges, awards, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F3` - Warm Cream / Linen):** The base canvas tone that infuses the entire interface with parchment-like warmth, avoiding clinical synthetic whites.

### Named Accent Colors
- **Terracotta Deep (`#B85D38`):** Pressed clay tone used for hover states on terracotta actions and urgent seasonal announcements.
- **Sage / Eucalyptus (`#7A8F6D`):** A lush botanical midtone for botanical tags, vegan/vegetarian badges, and ambient decorative elements.
- **Sage Subtle (`#E3E9DE`):** Ultra-soft eucalyptus wash for container fills, ingredient chips, and subtle focus backgrounds.
- **Tuscan Honey Light (`#FAEEDA`):** Muted honey tint for notice banners, chef recommendation highlights, and badge surfaces.
- **Espresso Ink (`#1B1C19`):** Deep roasted charcoal-brown for high-contrast, effortless editorial legibility on cream.
- **Parchment Border (`#DEDA0`):** Soft organic border tone for hairline dividers, card boundaries, and subtle frames.

## Typography

Typography strikes a dialogue between European literary heritage and contemporary transactional precision:

- **Headlines & Display (Playfair Display):** High-contrast transitional serifs offer an artisanal, editorial flair suited for cafe menus, heritage stories, and bakery feature items. Italic styling is embraced selectively for origin notes, tasting descriptors (e.g., *Notes of stone fruit & raw honey*), and seasonal dish highlights.
- **Body & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A friendly, warm geometric sans-serif with open counters and humanist traits that ensures effortless legibility across dense ingredient listings, reservation calendars, and cart checkouts.
- **Letter Spacing & Proportions:** Headline tokens utilize tight negative tracking for monumental impact, while small labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) use deliberate positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to ensure uppercase tags remain pristine and legible at micro-sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a fluid 12-column architectural grid with generous, unhurried white space that gives food photography and editorial storytelling room to resonate.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout bounded by a max-width container of `1280px`. Outer margins are set to `3rem` (`margin`) with a column gutter of `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`) outer margins and `1rem` (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Sticky booking footers and full-width sheet interactions replace multi-column sidebars.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Reserved for icon-to-label gaps, chip padding, and compact tag clusters.
- **Component Spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Governs card interior padding, menu item gaps, and standard form row spacing.
- **Macro Spacing (`space-xl` and multi-units):** Dictates hero band margins, culinary section separations, and generous editorial rhythm breaks.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids sterile synthetic shadows and excessive flat planes. Instead, depth is constructed through rich tonal layering, delicate organic outlines, and warm ambient light:

- **Tonal Layering:** The foundational background rests on warm linen (`#FBF9F3`). Card containers, side sheets, and menus sit at elevated warm cream tones (`#FFFFFF` or `#F5F3EE`), providing subtle paper-on-linen separation.
- **Ambient Botanical Shadows:** Shadows are tinted with deep olive and warm espresso pigments rather than neutral greyscale, evoking natural morning sunlight filtering through bistro glass:
  - *Resting Surface:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(27, 28, 25, 0.03)`
  - *Floating / Hover Card:* `0 14px 28px -6px rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.09), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(192, 107, 62, 0.05)`
  - *Modals & Elevated Drawers:* `0 24px 48px -10px rgba(27, 28, 25, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.06)`
- **Low-Contrast Parchment Outlines:** Structural borders employ subtle `1px` hairlines (`#DEDA0` or `rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.12)`), providing tactile, craft-inspired containment without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects organic artisan craftsmanship—reminiscent of hand-thrown ceramic dishware, proved sourdough loaves, and smooth river pebbles.

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to buttons, inputs, segmented controls, and notification toasts.
- **Large Corner Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Used for menu display cards, bakery photo frames, reservation panels, and dialog surfaces.
- **Extra-Large Corner Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Reserved for hero imagery containers, seasonal feature spotlights, and modal sheets.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Applied exclusively to category filters, diet/allergen chips (e.g., Sourdough, Organic, Pastry), and floating action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Olive Action:** Solid `#3D4A30` background, `#FBF9F3` text, border none, radius `0.5rem`. On hover, transitions smoothly to `#27331B`.
- **Secondary Terracotta Action:** Solid `#C06B3E` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for "Reserve Table" or "Order Fresh Pastries". On hover, darkens to `#B85D38`.
- **Botanical Outlined:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#3D4A30`, text `#3D4A30`. On hover, fills with `rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.06)`.
- **Honey Accent Action:** Background `#D4A359`, text `#1B1C19` (espresso ink). Used for special event bookings or loyalty highlights.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Botanical / Herb Badge:** Background `#E3E9DE`, text `#3D4A30`, radius `9999px`, font `label-sm`. Used for botanical infusions, vegan, and local sourcing notes.
- **Terracotta Warm Badge:** Background `rgba(192, 107, 62, 0.12)`, text `#B85D38`, border `1px solid rgba(192, 107, 62, 0.25)`. Perfect for "Fresh from the Oven" or "Baker's Pick".
- **Honey Special Badge:** Background `#FAEEDA`, text `#8C6322`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.35)`.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with `1px` border in `#DEDA0`. Selected state transforms to solid `#3D4A30` fill with cream text.

### Cards & Menu Showcases
- **Menu Item Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F3EE`, bound by `1px` border in `#DEDA0`, with `1rem` radius and `1.5rem` interior padding. Photography features a subtle `0.5rem` inner radius.
- **Interactive Lift:** On hover, cards subtly translateY `-3px` with a combined olive and terracotta ambient shadow bloom.

### Lists & Menu Typography Rules
- **Classic Menu Layout:** Dish name styled in `title-md` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), followed by a delicate warm-toned dotted leader (`border-b border-dotted border-[#DEDA0]`), and the price aligned right in `Playfair Display` semibold.
- **Ingredient & Origin Descriptions:** Styled in `body-sm` using `rgba(27, 28, 25, 0.75)` with italic botanical notes in `#7A8F6D` (e.g., *Rosemary honey, cold-pressed olive oil*).

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Input Fields (Text, Date, Guest Select):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DEDA0`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Active focus applies a `1.5px` border in `#3D4A30` accompanied by a warm honey/olive glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked states feature a `1.5px` border in `#7A8F6D`. Checked states fill with `#3D4A30` with a linen `#FBF9F3` check icon.

### Reservation Calendar & Table Selector
- **Time Slots:** Segmented pills with `1px` border in `#DEDA0`. Hovering tints the slot with `#FAEEDA`. Active selection fills with solid `#C06B3E` (Terracotta) and white text, confirming time with warmth.
