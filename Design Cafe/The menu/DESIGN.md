## Brand & Style

This design system translates the quiet luxury and tactile warmth of an artisanal European cafe, specialty roastery, and patisserie into a digital medium. The atmosphere evokes early mornings in sunlit bistros, freshly ground single-origin coffee, warm sourdough, and linen aprons.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous cream-toned negative space balanced with deep, grounded botanical tones.
- Organic softness via curved geometries and refined hairline framing.
- Elevated editorial restraint prioritizing mouth-watering imagery and deliberate typography over loud micro-interactions.
- An emotional resonance that feels welcoming, refined, sensory, and unhurried.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from Mediterranean olive groves, milled grains, and specialty espresso extractions:

- **Primary (`#3D4A30` - Deep Olive):** Anchors headlines, primary actions, and structural accents. Provides deep organic contrast against light surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#5A6D44` - Warm Olive):** Used for hover states, interactive highlights, and secondary hierarchy elements.
- **Tertiary (`#8A9A74` - Sage):** Used for subtle badges, decorative borders, active state backgrounds, and secondary metadata.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF8F2` - Soft Linen):** The foundational background, replacing sterile white with a tactile, cream undertone.
- **Neutral Surface Accent (`#F3EFE6` - Warm Almond):** Used for cards, side panels, nested containers, and table backgrounds.
- **Text & Contrast (`#241E18` - Espresso):** The primary typography tone, softer than pure black, imparting an ink-on-linen editorial feel.

## Typography

The type scale balances editorial tradition with modern legibility:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the classical heritage of French and Italian bakeries. High stroke contrast demands ample line-height to breathe. Use italic variants sparingly for single evocative words, origins, or seasonal callouts.
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A humanist-leaning geometric sans-serif whose open apertures and gentle curves ensure optimal digital readability for menus, dietary tags, and transactional reservation flows.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with slight positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) in medium or semibold weights to maintain legibility at smaller scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured yet generous 12-column grid that expands into unconfined, breathable whitespace:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive layout capped at a maximum container width of `1280px`. Outer margin is `3rem` (`space-xl` + `space-sm`), column gutter is `1.5rem` (`gutter`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`) outer margins and `1rem` (`gutter-mobile`) gutters.

Component internal padding scales predictably:
- Compact elements (badges, buttons, small chips) use `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Form inputs, cards, and list rows rely on `space-md` and `space-lg`.
- Hero sections, section dividers, and menu category breaks require generous vertical breathing room (`space-xl` through double `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, instead creating physical depth through warm tonal surfaces, ambient tinted shadows, and delicate borders:

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#FAF8F2` (Linen). Floating surfaces, cards, and modals sit at `#F3EFE6` (Almond) or pure `#FFFFFF` to suggest natural parchment resting on wood.
- **Ambient Olive Shadows:** When elevation is required (such as sticky reservation bars, modals, or hovering menu cards), use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with olive pigments rather than neutral black:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.04)`
  - *Elevated / Floating Card:* `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.04)`
  - *Modals & Drawers:* `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(36, 30, 24, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(36, 30, 24, 0.06)`
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Surfaces are framed with subtle hairlines in `rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.12)` or `rgba(138, 154, 116, 0.25)` to reinforce crisp craftsmanship.

## Shapes

The design system employs a roundedness index of `2` (medium-soft), evoking artisanal organic forms—such as handcrafted pottery, smooth river stones, and baked goods:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for input fields, buttons, interactive tags, and toasts.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to menu cards, gallery tiles, modal dialogues, and reservation widgets.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / `24px`):** Reserved for hero image frames, editorial pull-quotes, and specialty promo banners.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Selectively reserved for category pills, dietary tags (e.g., Vegan, Gluten-Free), and round floating action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#3D4A30` (Deep Olive), text `#FAF8F2` (Linen), border none, radius `0.5rem`. On hover: `#5A6D44` (Warm Olive) with smooth `150ms` ease.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent background, `1px` border in `#3D4A30`, text `#3D4A30`. On hover: subtle background tint `rgba(90, 109, 68, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost:** Text `#3D4A30`, no border. On hover: text `#5A6D44` with a subtle bottom underline animation.

### Cards
- **Menu & Bakery Cards:** Background `#F3EFE6` or `#FFFFFF`, `1px` border in `rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.12)`, radius `1rem`. Padding `1.5rem`. Image containers within cards should feature `0.75rem` radius with an inner inset border.
- **Interactive State:** Cards lift slightly (`-2px` transform) with an ambient tinted olive shadow transition.

### Chips & Badges
- **Dietary & Origin Tags:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, background `rgba(138, 154, 116, 0.15)`, text `#3D4A30`, font style `label-sm`.
- **Selected Filter Chips:** Solid `#3D4A30` background with `#FAF8F2` text.

### Form Inputs
- **Fields (Text, Date, Select):** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F2`, border `1px solid rgba(61, 74, 48, 0.2)`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, typography `body-md`. Focus state uses `1.5px solid #3D4A30` and a `3px` outer ring in `rgba(90, 109, 68, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Accented with `#3D4A30`. Unchecked border `1.5px solid #8A9A74`. Checked fills with `#3D4A30` with a linen `#FAF8F2` checkmark icon.

### Menu Lists & Price Groupings
- **List Items:** Menu items feature item name in `title-md`, a delicate dotted leader line (`border-b border-dotted border-stone-300`), and price aligned right in `Playfair Display` medium weight.
- **Ingredient Descriptions:** Nested immediately below title using `body-sm` in `rgba(36, 30, 24, 0.7)`.

### Reservation Widget & Time Pickers
- **Time Slots:** Segmented buttons styled as pill or soft rectangles with `1px` border. Available slots feature `#FAF8F2` background; active selection inverts to `#3D4A30` background with cream text.
