Dietary Accommodations & Clean Kitchen Protocol

Our kitchen handles wheat, tree nuts, and dairy. However, we maintain dedicated stone-milled prep stations and sterilized espresso wands for oat milk extraction. Gluten-friendly sourdough tartines are prepared using separate cutting boards and toasters. Inform your host of any severe sensitivities upon arrival.
